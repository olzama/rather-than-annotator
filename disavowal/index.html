Guideline

Disavowal 1
The use calibrates the status, scope or strength of the authors' own claim, contribution, result, method, tool or artifact: it says what that thing is and is not. This works in either direction. It can limit the claim by declining a stronger, broader, more certain or more final status (a hypothesis rather than a tested mechanism; indicative rather than conclusive; intended to assist rather than replace). Or it can reject a limiting or deflating characterization of the authors' work (a general method rather than one calibration procedure; one piece of evidence rather than the definition). In both cases the sentence is about how the authors' own work should be read, not about which technical option was chosen.
Approach contrast 2
Contrasts methods, designs, models, data, metrics or procedures: what was done or chosen as opposed to another approach (the authors' own or others'), as a description of the technical choice.
Finding contrast 3
States what a finding, phenomenon or property is, as opposed to something else it might have been taken for (“the errors are systematic rather than random”); it asserts something about the findings.
Other 4
None of the above.
